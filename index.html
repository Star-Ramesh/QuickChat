QuickChat

Say it. Then it's gone.

Create a room, share the link, and chat directly between devices. No accounts and no saved history. When the room closes, everything in it is cleared.

About. QuickChat connects browsers with WebRTC through a free public PeerJS signaling server. Messages and media stay in RAM only while the room is open. The room lives only while its host stays online, and room rules are enforced by the host's browser, so they cannot be tamper-proof. Designed & developed by [STAR RAMESH].
